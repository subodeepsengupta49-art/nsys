🧮 Algebra (Class 10–12)

Introduction

Algebra studies symbols and the rules for manipulating those symbols. It provides the language and tools for solving equations, modeling relationships, and preparing for higher mathematics. This page is organized by syllabus level so students from Class 10, 11, and 12 can find focused lessons, formulas, worked examples, practice problems, and embedded videos.

Class 10 Topics

  • Polynomials: degree, zeroes, factorization, Remainder and Factor Theorems.
  • Linear equations: solving single-variable and simple systems; word problems.
  • Quadratic equations: standard form, discriminant, roots (factorization, completing square, quadratic formula).
  • Arithmetic progressions (AP): nth term, sum of first n terms.
  • Basic inequalities: solving and interpreting simple linear inequalities.

Class 11 Topics

  • Relations and functions: domain, range, types of functions (linear, polynomial, rational).
  • Sequences and series: AP, GP, sum formulas, convergence basics.
  • Complex numbers: a + bi form, modulus, conjugate, polar form introduction.
  • Binomial theorem: expansion for positive integer powers, binomial coefficients.
  • Matrices and determinants (intro): basic operations, 2×2 determinants, solving linear systems using determinants (Cramer's rule).

Class 12 Topics

  • Advanced polynomials: factorization techniques, remainder theorem applications, symmetric polynomials.
  • Advanced matrices: inverse, rank, solving larger linear systems, eigenconcepts (intro).
  • Determinants: properties, expansion, applications to area/volume and linear systems.
  • Sequences and series (advanced): convergence tests, sum to infinity for GP, introduction to power series.
  • Permutations and combinations: counting principles, binomial identities, basic probability connections.

Key Formulas and Identities

Topic Formula / Identity Notes
Quadratic formula \(x = \dfrac{-b \pm \sqrt{b^2 - 4ac}}{2a}\) Use discriminant \(D=b^2-4ac\) to determine roots
Sum of roots (quadratic) \(\alpha + \beta = -\dfrac{b}{a}\) From \(ax^2+bx+c=0\)
Product of roots (quadratic) \(\alpha\beta = \dfrac{c}{a}\) From \(ax^2+bx+c=0\)
AP nth term & sum \(a_n = a_1 + (n-1)d\); \(S_n = \dfrac{n}{2}(2a_1 + (n-1)d)\) Arithmetic progressions
GP nth term & sum \(a_n = a_1 r^{n-1}\); \(S_n = a_1 \dfrac{r^n - 1}{r - 1}\) (r≠1) Geometric progressions
Binomial theorem \((x+y)^n = \sum_{k=0}^{n} \binom{n}{k} x^{n-k} y^k\) Valid for integer \(n\ge0\)
Determinant 2×2 \(\det\begin{pmatrix}a & b \\ c & d\end{pmatrix} = ad - bc\) Used in area, inverse, Cramer's rule

Examples and Practice Problems

Example 1 — Factorization

Factorize \(x^3 - 6x^2 + 11x - 6\).

Step 1: Try integer roots 1,2,3
f(1)=0 → (x-1) is a factor
Divide polynomial by (x-1) → x^2 - 5x + 6
Factor quadratic → (x-2)(x-3)
Final: (x-1)(x-2)(x-3)

Example 2 — Quadratic word problem

Find two numbers whose sum is 13 and product is 40.

Let numbers be x and 13-x
x(13-x)=40 → 13x - x^2 = 40 → x^2 -13x + 40 = 0
Solve: Discriminant D = 169 - 160 = 9 → roots (13 ± 3)/2 → 8 and 5
Numbers: 8 and 5

Practice Set (with answers hidden)

  1. Factorize \(x^2 + 5x + 6\).
  2. Find sum of first 20 terms of AP with \(a_1=3\) and \(d=4\).
  3. Solve system: \(2x + 3y = 7\), \(x - y = 1\).
  4. Expand \((2x - 3)^3\) using binomial theorem.
Answers
  1. \((x+2)(x+3)\)
  2. \(S_{20} = \dfrac{20}{2}(2*3 + 19*4) = 10(6 + 76) = 820\)
  3. From second eqn x = y + 1 → substitute: 2(y+1) + 3y = 7 → 5y + 2 = 7 → y = 1 → x = 2
  4. \((2x - 3)^3 = 8x^3 - 36x^2 + 54x - 27\)
