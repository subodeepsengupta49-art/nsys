About SU80

Built and maintained by Subodeep Sengupta — focused on clear, syllabus-aligned math learning for Class 10–12.

Who we are

SU80 is an educational web project created to make mathematics approachable and exam-ready for students in Classes 10, 11, and 12. The site provides concise notes, worked examples, practice problems, quick-reference tables, and short embedded lessons so learners can study efficiently.

Founder & Developer

Mission

Our mission is to make mathematics clear, practical, and accessible. We focus on step-by-step explanations, worked examples, and short videos so students can learn concepts quickly and apply them in exams and projects.

What we offer

  • Topic pages for Trigonometry, Algebra, Calculus, and Number Systems with syllabus-aligned content.
  • Embedded lessons — short videos and worked examples playable on the site.
  • Practice problems with step-by-step solutions and quick reference tables.
  • Responsive design so lessons work on phones, tablets, and desktops.

How to use SU80

  1. Open a topic from the navigation bar.
  2. Choose the class level (10 / 11 / 12) that matches your syllabus.
  3. Watch the short concept video, then try the worked examples and practice problems.
