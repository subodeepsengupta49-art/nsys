📐 Trigonometry (Class 10–12)

✨ Introduction

Trigonometry is the study of relationships between angles and sides of triangles. It is a core part of the mathematics curriculum in Classes 10, 11, and 12, with applications in geometry, physics, engineering, astronomy, and computer science.

📘 Class 10 Topics

  • Trigonometric ratios of acute angles (sin, cos, tan, sec, csc, cot).
  • Trigonometric identities: sin²θ + cos²θ = 1, etc.
  • Heights and distances problems using trigonometry.

📗 Class 11 Topics

  • Measurement of angles in degrees and radians.
  • Signs of trigonometric functions in different quadrants.
  • Graphs of sine, cosine, and tangent functions.
  • Trigonometric identities: sum, difference, double angle, and triple angle formulas.

📙 Class 12 Topics

  • Inverse trigonometric functions and their properties.
  • Applications of trigonometry in calculus (limits, differentiation, integration).
  • Solving trigonometric equations.
  • Use of trigonometry in vectors and 3D geometry.

✨ Conclusion

Trigonometry builds a bridge between algebra and geometry, and is essential for higher studies in mathematics, science, and engineering.
